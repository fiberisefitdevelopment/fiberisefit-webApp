==========================================

Q: What is Fiberise Fit?
A: Fiberise Fit is an Indian health supplement brand. Our hero product is FYBER — a science-backed, stimulant-free fiber supplement for appetite control, craving reduction, gut health, and sustainable weight management. We ship Pan-India from New Delhi.

Q: What is FYBER?
A: FYBER is a dietary supplement powered by LEAN-X, a patent-pending blend of soluble fiber, prebiotics, probiotics, L-Carnitine L-Tartrate, and L-Tyrosine. It works by forming a gentle gel in your stomach that signals fullness to your brain, helping you eat less naturally — without stimulants or prescription hormones.

Q: What makes FYBER different from regular fiber supplements?
A: Unlike basic fiber supplements (like plain psyllium), FYBER combines Glucomannan, Chicory Root Inulin, Lactobacillus gasseri probiotic, L-Carnitine, and L-Tyrosine. It targets both satiety (feeling full) and gut microbiome health for sustained metabolic support.

Q: Is FYBER like Ozempic?
A: No. Ozempic (semaglutide) is a prescription injection that chemically mimics GLP-1 hormone. FYBER is a natural fiber supplement that helps your body release its own satiety signals (GLP-1 and PYY) through gastric distension — safely, without systemic drug side effects. It is NOT a substitute for prescription medication.

SECTION 3 — INGREDIENTS & HOW TO USE
==========================================

Q: What are the ingredients in FYBER?
A: FYBER's LEAN-X blend contains:
- Soluble dietary fiber (Glucomannan)
- Chicory Root Extract (Inulin) — prebiotic fiber
- Lactobacillus gasseri — probiotic
- L-Carnitine L-Tartrate (LCLT) — supports fat metabolism
- L-Tyrosine — supports energy and mental clarity
Product is: Third-party tested, Non-GMO, Vegan, cruelty-free

Q: How do I take FYBER?
A: Mix 1 sachet in 200–300 ml of water. Drink it 20–30 minutes before your largest meal (usually lunch or dinner). Drink an extra glass of water after. You can take 1–2 sachets per day.

Q: When will I feel the effects?
A: Many users notice reduced cravings within about 30 minutes of taking FYBER. Visible changes typically appear over weeks:
- 30 days: Better portion control, fewer cravings
- 30–60 days: Improved digestion, less bloating
- 60–90+ days: Sustainable weight management, stronger metabolic foundation

Q: Do I need to diet or exercise?
A: No strict diet is required. FYBER works best with balanced eating and moderate activity, but it naturally helps you eat less by controlling cravings and portion sizes.

Q: Can I take FYBER long-term?
A: Yes. FYBER is made from natural food-based fibers with zero stimulants or habit-forming compounds. It is safe for continuous daily use.

Q: Does FYBER have side effects?
A: When used as directed with adequate water, FYBER has no known serious side effects. Ingredients are recognized as safe (GRAS by US FDA, EFSA in Europe). Like any fiber supplement, drink enough water.

Q: Will I feel low energy while taking FYBER?
A: No. FYBER contains L-Carnitine L-Tartrate and L-Tyrosine, which support energy metabolism and mental clarity — especially helpful during a calorie deficit.

Q: Will I gain weight back after stopping FYBER?
A: Not necessarily. FYBER helps improve gut health and appetite regulation over time. Many users naturally eat smaller portions even after stopping. Maintaining balanced eating habits helps sustain results.

==========================================

Q: Is FYBER safe for women and hormones?
A: Yes. Dietary fiber supports metabolic balance and can indirectly support hormonal regulation. FYBER is generally safe for women.

Q: Can I take FYBER with PCOS, thyroid, diabetes, hypertension, high cholesterol, or fatty liver?
A: FYBER's soluble fiber helps slow glucose absorption, which benefits people managing PCOS, insulin resistance, and diabetes. It is generally safe, but if you are on prescription medication or under medical treatment, please consult your doctor before starting any supplement.

Q: Can pregnant or breastfeeding women take FYBER?
A: Please consult your obstetrician or doctor before taking any supplement during pregnancy or breastfeeding.

Q: Can I lose weight just by taking FYBER?
A: FYBER helps by reducing hunger and cravings, which naturally leads to eating less. A calorie deficit is the primary driver of weight loss. FYBER makes it easier to maintain that deficit without strict dieting.

Q: Is FYBER a medicine?
A: No. FYBER is a dietary supplement, not a drug or prescription product. It does not treat, cure, or prevent any disease. Always consult a healthcare professional for medical advice.
