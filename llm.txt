# Fiberise Fit

> Fiberise Fit is an Indian health supplement brand that makes FYBER — a science-backed, stimulant-free fiber supplement for appetite control, craving reduction, gut health, and sustainable weight management. FYBER is powered by LEAN-X®, a patented multi-action formula that activates the body's natural GLP-1 pathways through soluble fiber, prebiotics, probiotics, L-Carnitine L-Tartrate, and L-Tyrosine. The brand is endorsed by doctors from AIIMS New Delhi, UNDP, and other leading healthcare institutions. Fiberise Fit ships across India from its registered office in Karol Bagh, New Delhi.

Fiberise Fit was founded to address a gap in the Indian weight management market: supplements that rely on stimulants, harsh appetite suppressants, or injections (like Ozempic/semaglutide). FYBER takes a physiological approach — using soluble fiber to create gastric distension, trigger GLP-1 and PYY hormone release, slow gastric emptying, stabilise blood sugar, and modulate appetite through the gut-brain axis. The formula is not habit-forming, requires no prescription, and is designed for long-term daily use.
